Arrays are a powerful data structure in JavaScript that can hold multiple values of different types. They provide various methods for manipulation and iteration.

In the examples above, we have seen how to create arrays, access their elements, and use various array methods such as push, pop, map, filter, and reduce. We also explored multidimensional arrays and arrays of objects. Arrays are essential for handling collections of data in JavaScript and are widely used in web development.

Arrays can be used to store lists of items, such as names, numbers, or even complex objects. They allow us to perform operations like sorting, searching, and transforming data efficiently.
